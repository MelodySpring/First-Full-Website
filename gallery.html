Gallery

Every moment in the wild tells a story - the sweep of wings over open water, the quiet pause of a heron in the shallows, the sudden burst of movement as a flock lifts into the air. This gallery brings together those fleeting encounters with water birds and the wider wildlife that inhabits our wetlands, coastlines and natural spaces. From avocets and egrets to seals, seabirds, and the many creatures that share these environments, each image is a reminder that remarkable nature thrives all around us. You don’t need to travel far to witness something extraordinary; often it’s waiting along a riverbank, across a tidal flat, or just beyond the reeds.
