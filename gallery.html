Every moment in the wild tells a story — a flash of movement, a quiet glance, a breath of stillness before the world shifts again. This gallery brings together those fleeting encounters: the birds that skim the hedgerows at dawn, the foxes that slip through the fields at dusk, and the small, often‑overlooked creatures that share our landscape. Each image is a reminder that extraordinary wildlife exists all around us, not just in far‑flung places, but right here on our doorstep. All photographs in this gallery are copyright protected.
