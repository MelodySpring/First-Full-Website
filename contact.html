Contact Me!

Contact Us Whether you’re looking to purchase one of my images, book a personalised photoshoot, or simply ask a question, I’m always happy to hear from you. Photography is at its best when it connects people — when a moment, a landscape, or a fleeting expression resonates with someone else. If something in my work has caught your eye, this is the place to reach out.

If you’d like to buy a print, licence an image, or enquire about digital usage, just let me know which photograph you’re interested in. I offer a range of print sizes, finishes, and bespoke options, and I’m always happy to help you choose the right format for your space or project.

For those interested in booking a photoshoot, I provide tailored sessions designed around you — whether it’s portraits, wildlife‑inspired creative work, product photography, or a shoot for your brand. Share a little about what you have in mind, and I’ll get back to you with availability, ideas, and pricing.

You can contact me using the form on this page or by email at john_melody@outlook.com. I aim to reply as quickly as possible, usually within 24 hours. No question is too small — whether you’re planning something big or just exploring possibilities, I’m here to help.

I look forward to hearing from you and creating something memorable together.
